October 13, 2007

The Brave One

Jodie Foster's character, playing vigilante. After being brutally attacked and losing her fiance, she got herself a gun. Trouble came looking for her and she had some target practise. What I find it strange is that New York finest does not have a forensics unit. There were no fingerprints or hairs to correlate between the different crime scenes.

The finale, well any regular viewer of CSI will tear that apart in 5 minutes and even without the use of DNA testing.

Aside from the above, an enjoyable movie, an apt lesson for any aspiring vigilante. Violence bites back.
