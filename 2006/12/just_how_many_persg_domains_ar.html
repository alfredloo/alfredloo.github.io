Just how many .Per.sg domains are there?

PER.sg is a very Singpaorean phenomenon. Invented by IDA to increase the number of personal webpages without all the papework required of COM.sg or even stricter ORG.sg I have always wondered if there are any other websites on .per.sg domain. So running a Google search reveals all.

There are lots of PER.sg websites. Religous websites, mainly Christians, a few commercial websites like a family physician and a unique photographer.

At the same time, there are numerous examples dormant and inactive websites. People testing out blog technology in Mar 06 and a few budding website designers with aborted projectes. Perhaps after the novelty wore off, they found the maintenance, a drag rather than a joy.
