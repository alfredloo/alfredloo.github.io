Hari Raya Puasa celebration

Took Ted to a colleagues' Hari Raya Celebration. Every year, to celebrate the Malay New Year, big do is organised at her house. Her family will invite all the relatives, friends and colleagues over for lunch or dinner. Everyone would pack into her house and tuck in on her mom's cooking.

It was raining really heavily in that afternoon. I thought I will not be able to make it. Not so nice not attend since I am her supervisor. Athena is in her Mommy mode, so I took Ted along.

Turned out he was ok with the crowd, but he preferred to keep me in sight. Fed him some rice, he was ok with it. Got a bit grouchy and decorated my white shirt with some curry. Decided to take my leave before he does any serious damage to some of the souvniers he fancy.
