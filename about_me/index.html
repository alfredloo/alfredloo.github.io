About me

I am an IT professional, specializing on Mail systems, mainly Exchange and Lotus Notes. I am also familiar with all versions Microsoft Windows operating systems, SQL 2000 server, ISA 2004 and various brands of firewalls.

I have worked 10 years in various industries and user environment, ranging from end user, distributor to system integrators.

I have a degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from University of Queensland. My thesis was on designing a microcoded microprocessor using Alliance VHDL. I am certified MCSE Messaging Specialist and Lotus Professional. The last certification I completed and certified was the HP Accredited System Engineer (ASE) ProLiant Servers c-Class Blades in August 2007.

My current interest is in virtualization.

I am also a husband and father to 2 precocious kids. I used to do hashing but family time takes all time.

You can contact me at alfred dot loo dot 2000 at gmail dot com for a detailed resume or any other matters.
